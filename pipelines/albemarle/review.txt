The current Mapbox style uses TrailName to assign official park trail colors. A name change can therefore alter the rendered color even if geometry is unchanged. Preserve curated name/color associations through explicit per-feature overrides. Track linked official maps as separate sources once registered. Do not assume that a new park or trail listed in the directory is represented in the current GIS data. County pages may block automated clients: report the failure rather than claiming there were no changes.
