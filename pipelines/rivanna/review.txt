Prioritize detours, new connections, closures, bridge work, and trail work events that affect access. Ignore fundraising and old blog posts unless they describe an unresolved current access issue. A change to an embedded map link should be flagged for map review. Do not treat Strava usage as permission to use a trail. Do not infer reopening from the removal of a notice.

The official Google My Maps KML export is monitored as text evidence. It has no stable placemark IDs and mixes routes, signs, parking, and an event-specific Loop deVille layer. It is not an automatic replacement dataset. Establish an explicit identity crosswalk, exclude event-only layers, and preserve curated route names/categories before proposing geometry. The PDF URLs are versioned: check the map page for replacement links as well as monitoring the registered files.
