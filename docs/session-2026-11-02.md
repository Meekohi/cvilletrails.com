Ownership does not mean erasing all features of other datasets within a rectangular park extent. Consolidation clips reviewed competing geometry at actual boundaries, retains outside portions and checks connections. Nearby parallel trails can be distinct routes. Overlap distances and coverage metrics help review; they do not decide identity on their own.

### Heyward saddle connector

The user clarified that the connector should join the northernmost points of the three networks, northeast of Round Top, without continuing down toward Round Top. The supplied points were:

| Point | Latitude | Longitude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 38.032501 | -78.552536 |
| 2 | 38.032269 | -78.550488 |
| 3 | 38.032699 | -78.548636 |

GeoJSON stores these in the opposite coordinate order: `[longitude, latitude]`. The policy binds the edit to Strava feature `8d36129f3e99bffe6d1807c85f373009` and the reviewed source geometry. The accepted line snaps to the relevant endpoints/vertex within about five meters of the supplied points and retains the original northeastern tail from the reviewed join. Those snaps are intentional; do not replace them with unsnapped coordinates just because the table appears more exact.

The ownership policy preserves the reviewed replacement and its source-feature hash. Recheck the joins visually if later City geometry moves or its feature identities change; this is not a general automatic topology repair. Separately, the City adapter has an explicit connection rule for the Quarry/Avon bridge. Investigate a constraint failure rather than removing the constraint to make an import succeed.

## Research findings to carry forward

The detailed [source guide](source-research.md) records endpoints, semantics and source-specific pitfalls. The important changes to our understanding were:

- A usable County GIS trail service **does exist**. The earlier audit that had not found one remains historical evidence, not the latest conclusion.
- Complete retrieval returned **1,902 records**. A naive first request returned only 1,000 without a helpful truncation flag. Explicit ID enumeration and batched completeness checks are necessary.
- The County service mixes active/inactive/future/pending trails and public/private or unknown ownership. Its field names and park names differ from the old curated dataset. A dedicated adapter and stable-ID crosswalk are still needed.
- The new GIS informed context envelopes for the seven regional infoboxes. It did not replace Mapbox trail geometry. Biscuit Run coverage is visibly marked incomplete.
- The initial visitor-info check established 20 source baselines and hit one UVA 403. All 14 directly fetched County pages in the later regional audit returned 403. Official indexed/browser evidence supported the descriptions, but those pages are not yet reliable unattended monitors.
- Official information can disagree or have a narrower scope than expected: Washington Park closing times conflict, Saunders–Monticello boardwalk dog rules differ from other trail areas, Montpelier access has admission conditions, and Wintergreen office hours are not trail opening hours. Missing or conflicting rules were not filled in by inference.
- Large isolated trail destinations take priority over adding every small urban park context. Crozet is a useful next investigation, but public/private/future greenway status needs review first.

## Deployment and caching lessons

The old Calendar button persisted because the website deployment was not publishing the updated branch/build. Commit `d13256a` corrected deployment from `master` through GitHub Actions, retaining the custom domain and HTTPS. Website assets and Mapbox tiles have separate publication paths.

The first Mapbox export exposed another distinction: upload success did not guarantee the internal vector layer name matched the style. The publisher now builds explicit named MBTiles at zooms 0–16. The account rejected a maximum of 18 during the session. Dataset verification also handles the observed six-decimal truncation of newly written coordinates without accepting arbitrary drift.

After the Rivanna publication completed, ordinary public zoom-10 tiles still lacked the new closure status and road walks while zooms 12, 14 and 16 showed them. A unique query key on tile requests returned current zoom-10/11 data with the same public style. The observed headers allowed two hours in shared caches and twelve hours in browsers. This was a cache difference at the sampled edge, not proof of an incomplete upload or worldwide propagation.

The [sanitized cache evidence](evidence/2026-11-03-mapbox-cache.json) preserves those observations. The [operations guide](mapbox-operations.md) explains how to repeat the diagnosis, resume interrupted publication and distinguish dataset, tileset, style and website failures. A completed upload API record does not report which cached tiles every visitor will receive.
